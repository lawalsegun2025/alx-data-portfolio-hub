About the Portfolio Hub

Why it exists, who built it, and where it's going.

Why This Exists

When ALX Data Programs moved to a self-paced learning model, something unexpected happened: learners who finished courses early had nowhere clear to go next. The most common question Technical Mentorship heard became: "What project should I build?"

This question was being answered one conversation at a time, over and over. That's not sustainable, and it's not the best use of anyone's time.

The Portfolio Hub was created to give every learner — regardless of when they finish, what industry they're interested in, or what domain they want to specialise in — a structured, high-quality place to keep building after the course ends.

The Design Philosophy

The Hub is a pool, not an assignment list. Learners choose based on what they're interested in, not what they've been told to do. That choice matters — it's how you build a portfolio that reflects you, not just the course you completed.

All projects use real-world data. No synthetic datasets. Real problems, real data, real decisions — because that's what prepares you for a real job.

Projects are organised by domain, not geography. You'll find projects in healthcare, agriculture, finance, energy, and more — relevant to learners across Africa and the world.

Who Built This

This pilot was designed and built by the Technical Mentorship function within ALX Data Programs, as part of the programme's implementation of a Portfolio Project Repository initiative approved by Data Programs leadership in July 2026.

Initiative Owner: Segun Lawal, Data Scientist & Technical Mentor, ALX Data Programs

Pilot Status

This site is a Technical Mentorship pilot prototype. It is not an officially launched ALX Africa platform. It has been built to validate the concept, gather learner feedback, and demonstrate the initiative to the wider Data Programs team and the Learning Experience Design team. Following validation, it may be integrated into the Savanna LMS or moved to an official ALX domain.

What's Coming Next

The current pilot focuses on Data Analytics (DA-2, DA-3, DA-4, and Capstone). Future phases will expand into:

  • Data Science — Python, machine learning, NLP, and classification projects
  • Data Engineering — pipeline, architecture, and infrastructure projects
  • Additional specialisations as ALX Data Programs expand

The repository is designed to grow — not stop at 30 projects. As new datasets, industries, and business problems emerge, the Hub will grow with them.

Feedback

This is a pilot. Your feedback makes it better. If you find a broken link, a confusing instruction, or a project that doesn't work as described — please tell us. And if you complete a project and want to share it, we'd love to hear.
